\subsubsection*{Molecular neighbourhood information beyond shared normalization}
A weaker raw correlation alone does not establish that the normalized association is artificial. We repeat the original control with 5,000 joint test-label permutations (seed 20260914), applying the same molecule permutation to all 13 targets and both architecture proxies. This preserves target distributions, IQRs, and cross-target relationships while disrupting label alignment with molecular neighbourhoods. Graphs, weights, and mean GNN test errors remain fixed; no GNN is retrained. DFTB is excluded. These controls are retrospective, not preregistered.

For both models, the observed signed Pearson correlation of \(\ln D_{\rm IQR}\) with test \(n\)-MAE exceeds all 5,000 shuffled values: one-sided plus-one \(p=1/5001\approx0.00020\), conditional on this panel and randomization scheme, without adjustment for the two architecture comparisons. High shuffled median \(R^2\) values (GemNet: 0.862; SchNet: 0.855) show that target distributions and normalization contribute substantially. The stronger observed associations support additional information from label--geometry alignment beyond this conditional null, not a purely physical explanation or universal learnability law. The observed-minus-shuffled \(R^2\) is not a decomposition of explained variance.
